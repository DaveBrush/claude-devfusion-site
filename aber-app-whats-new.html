October update — in beta now

The app just had a proper seaside makeover.

Same festival, same laughs — but the app finally keeps up. New look, real-time group planning, and the map's where you'd actually expect it. Here's what changed.

6New things

We rebuilt a fair chunk of the app for this festival — not because it was broken, but because "good enough" isn't really the standard when the whole point is not missing the show everyone's talking about on Monday.

Six things are properly new. Scroll on for the rundown.

Interface

A new look, top to bottom

We tore up the old screens and started again — a hub that finally looks like it belongs to a comedy festival by the sea, clearer type throughout, and a layout that doesn't fight you in a dark venue at 11pm.

Groups

Plan together, in real time

Start a room, share the six-letter code, and watch your group's picks land on your screen the moment someone taps favourite. No more "wait, are we doing the 8pm or the 9:30?" texts.

Tickets

Know what's actually booked

Favouriting a show and having a ticket for it are different things — the app finally agrees. Mark what you've bought and it shows up everywhere, so your plans and your bookings are on the same page.

Schedule

See your whole weekend at a glance

Switch to the timetable view to see every show laid out by venue and hour — then filter it down to just your favourites, just what's booked, or just what your group's going to.

Venues

Every venue, every show

Tap into a venue and see its full running order — handy for working out what's on next door once you've necked your interval coffee.

Maps

The map found a better home

It used to live behind a button on the home screen; now it's built into every venue page, so you can see exactly where you're headed without leaving what you were looking at.

Android only for now — iOS is coming, sorry

It's in beta — go join it.

The redesign isn't in the app stores yet. It's live in open beta on Android, and you can jump in today — opt in on the web, or head straight to the Play Store listing if you're on your phone.
